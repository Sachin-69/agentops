# Billing and Subscriptions

## Plans
We offer three plans:
- **Free**: up to 3 projects and community support.
- **Pro**: $12 per user per month, unlimited projects, and email support.
- **Enterprise**: custom pricing with SSO, audit logs, and a dedicated manager.

## Changing your plan
To upgrade or downgrade, go to **Settings > Billing > Change Plan**. Upgrades
take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the end of
the current billing cycle.

## Refund policy
We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all Pro subscriptions. To request a
refund, contact billing support from the account you were charged on. Refunds
are processed to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.

## Accepted payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and PayPal. Enterprise customers
may also pay by invoice and bank transfer.

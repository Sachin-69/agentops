# Getting Started

## Creating an account
To create an account, click **Sign Up** on the top right of the homepage, enter
your email address and a password, and confirm your email through the link we
send you. Accounts are free and take about two minutes to set up.

## Logging in
Go to the login page and enter the email and password you registered with. If
you have two-factor authentication enabled, you will also be prompted for a code
from your authenticator app.

## System requirements
Our web app supports the latest two versions of Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and
Safari. A stable internet connection is recommended. There is no desktop app;
everything runs in the browser.
